# Company Context

Use this file for strategic and messaging context that should influence project rationale, scope framing, and KPI selection.

## Mission and positioning
- MyVeloFit is focused on improving the cycling experience through accessible, data-driven bike fitting and sizing.
- Product value should stay anchored to rider comfort, performance, confidence, and accessibility.

## Company values
- Prioritize the cyclist.
- Cycling for everyone.
- Act with integrity.
- Innovate continuously.
- Strengthen communities.

## Growth context
- Revenue and user growth are active priorities.
- The long-range direction is category leadership in digital bike fitting and sizing.
- Strategy references emphasize localization, referral and affiliate growth, and expansion of core fit-related capabilities.

## Product context
- B2C focus: guided self-serve sizing and fitting, optional expert help, comfort and performance outcomes.
- B2B focus: fitters, shops, and training centers using the platform to scale expertise and deliver better service.

## Marketing and KPI themes
- Primary growth themes include sign-ups, conversion to paid, educational engagement, creator and community presence, and event visibility.
- Product and customer success themes include time to first fit, fit quality, satisfaction, and reduced friction.

## How to use this context
- Use it to explain why a project matters now.
- Tie goals to rider benefit first, then business impact.
- Prefer KPIs that can be measured during rollout and after launch.
- Avoid copying strategy language verbatim when a simpler formulation fits the project better.
