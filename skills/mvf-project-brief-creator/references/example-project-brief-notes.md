# Example Project Brief Notes

Source: `assets/source-pdfs/Example Project Brief.pdf`

Use this file for stylistic cues, not as a rigid template.

## Formatting signals from the example
- The title includes a numeric prefix and project name.
- Sections are clearly labeled and kept short.
- Background explains both evidence and strategic intent.
- Scope mixes concrete deliverables with items under investigation.
- KPI and success sections separate operational tracking from broader impact.
- Timeline content is milestone-oriented rather than a dense schedule.

## Recommended reuse
- Short opening summary paragraph.
- Bulleted drivers or opportunities in the background section.
- Clear phase framing for design, build, test, launch, and follow-up.

## Avoid overfitting
- Do not copy wording from the example.
- Adjust structure within sections to match the size and certainty of the project.
