# Project Brief Template

Use this exact section order unless the user requests a variant.

## 1. TL;DR Summary
- One short paragraph covering the project, target outcome, and expected value.

## 2. Background
- Explain why the work matters now.
- Connect the effort to user need, strategic direction, growth goals, or observed friction.

## 3. Scope and Objectives
- List in-scope deliverables or features.
- Call out out-of-scope items or items still under investigation.

## 4. Project Overview
- Describe the delivery approach by phase where useful.
- Include likely owners such as PM, design, engineering, data, support, or marketing when known.

## 5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Name the rollout and monitoring metrics the team should track.
- Favor measurable operational metrics over vague aspirations.

## 6. Success Metrics
- State the business or user impact that would indicate the initiative worked.
- This can overlap with KPIs, but should emphasize the outcome rather than the instrument panel.

## 7. Timeline and Milestones
- Include dates when known.
- Use `[Update Required]` for undefined dates, owners, or dependencies.

## Authoring rules
- Keep headings stable.
- Use bullets inside sections when they make scope or metrics easier to scan.
- Do not fabricate dates, owners, launch scope, or KPI baselines.
