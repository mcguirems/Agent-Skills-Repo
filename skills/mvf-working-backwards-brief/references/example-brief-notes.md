## Most representative tone
- The Expert Review brief is the closest fit for the intended voice: clear, direct, useful, and believable.

## Reusable patterns
- Headline and subheading focus on rider benefit before technical detail.
- Problem paragraphs explain friction in simple customer terms.
- Solution paragraphs describe how the workflow fits naturally into a rider's process.
- FAQs stay short and concrete.
- Internal FAQs are where rollout nuance, support detail, and launch caveats belong.

## What to avoid
- Overstated visionary language.
- Dense technical explanation in the public press release.
- Customer quotes that sound generic or overly polished.
