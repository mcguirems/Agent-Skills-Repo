# Company Context

Use this file when positioning a launch in external-facing or internal-facing language.

## About MyVeloFit
MyVeloFit is an AI-driven online bike fitting and sizing platform that helps cyclists improve comfort, performance, and confidence from home or through fitter-led workflows.

## Framing priorities
- Rider benefit comes first.
- Keep claims practical and easy to understand.
- Favor clear explanations over jargon.
- Tie product value to comfort, performance, convenience, confidence, and better decision-making.

## Audience lenses
- B2C: self-serve cyclists seeking a better ride, easier setup, and stronger buying or fit decisions.
- B2B: fitters, shops, and training centers seeking scalable expertise, efficient service delivery, and ongoing customer value.

## Writing guidance
- Use a friendly but professional tone.
- Avoid buzzwords and unsupported superlatives unless they are already part of approved positioning.
- If a feature is future-facing, present it as planned or upcoming rather than already live.
