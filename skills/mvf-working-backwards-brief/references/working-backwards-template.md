# Working Backwards Template

Use this structure for every Working Backwards brief.

## 1. Press Release

### Heading
- Name the product or launch in customer language.

### Subheading
- One sentence naming the target user and the main value.

### Summary paragraph
- Start with city and launch date context.
- Summarize what is launching and why it matters.

### Problem paragraph
- Describe the customer problem from the rider's or buyer's point of view.

### Solution paragraph(s)
- Explain how MyVeloFit solves the problem.
- Include the practical workflow, key capabilities, and intended outcome.

### Internal quote
- Use a credible company spokesperson.

### Customer quote
- Make it realistic and specific.

### Getting started
- Explain how a user tries, buys, activates, or learns more about the feature.

### About and contact
- Include standard company framing and media contact when appropriate.

## 2. Customer FAQ
- Keep answers short and user-friendly.
- Cover availability, who it is for, what it does, onboarding, pricing or plans if relevant, and limitations if they matter to the experience.

## 3. Internal FAQ
- Cover rollout approach, dependencies, support readiness, data handling, risks, roadmap edges, and unresolved decisions.

## Authoring rules
- Write the press release first, then customer FAQ, then internal FAQ.
- Do not hide uncertainty. Mark unknown launch facts explicitly.
- Make the content consistent with the source project brief.
